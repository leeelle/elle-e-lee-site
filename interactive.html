As the lead digital designer on Harry Winston’s creative team, I designed campaign pages with Valtech. These three projects cover a high jewelry collection, destination-led product pages and a holiday e-card.

Talk to Me,
Harry Winston

High Jewelry Collection · 2026

Large images and a simple page structure

Talk to Me, Harry Winston draws on the brand’s red-carpet history and Fifth Avenue salon. I designed the page around large views of the jewelry and a restrained layout.

Each section gives a piece and its story space before introducing the next.

Scroll reveals

Each chapter begins with a quotation in large type. As the visitor scrolls, the jewelry is revealed behind the text. The interaction connects the quotation to the piece it describes.

An editorial layout

The page follows a repeated structure: jewelry, title and a short description. The consistent layout makes room for different pieces without adding competing banners or product grids.

Editorial and workshop imagery

I paired images of the finished jewelry with workshop footage. The overlapping layout brings the campaign imagery and the making of the pieces into the same view.

The page uses scale, spacing and gradual reveals to give visitors time to examine each piece.

Majestic Escapes

High Jewelry Collection · 2025

From destination to jewelry

Majestic Escapes takes its references from ten destinations, including Seville, Venice, Tahiti and Oahu.

I designed the pages to introduce each destination before showing the jewelry it inspired.

A consistent structure for each destination

Each chapter begins with the location and its colors. The layout stays consistent while the imagery changes, keeping the collection connected across all ten destinations.

Film to product view

The product page opens with campaign footage of the model, followed by a rotating view of the jewelry. The piece then settles beside its description.

Product zoom

I added a zoom view so visitors could examine the stones and settings in more detail.

Each sequence ends with a close view of the jewelry.

Holiday E-card

Visitors could choose a card, write a message and share it. I designed the sequence and the transitions between steps.

How I work

Prototypes and build reviews

For each project, I prototyped the key interactions in Figma, handed the screens and motion specifications to Valtech’s developers, and reviewed the builds against the design before launch.
