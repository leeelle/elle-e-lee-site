Holiday

Creative direction · 2023 · CGI with The Mill

CGI film and campaign assets

The Holiday campaign follows the Winston Express through a snowy village, carrying a Harry Winston box.

We developed the CGI film with The Mill, then used stills from it for the printed catalog and other campaign formats. Building the scenes once in CGI meant a single production could supply the film, the catalog and the rest of the campaign.
