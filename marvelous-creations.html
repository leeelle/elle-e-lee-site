Marvelous Creations is a Harry Winston high jewelry collection inspired by nature. The campaign placed the pieces in a museum setting, where creatures emerged from their frames as jewelry.

I led creative direction for Part 1 in 2021 and Part 2 in 2025. For Part 2, I used AI to develop visual references before production.

The problem

Designing before the jewelry was finished

The campaign needed to be developed while the one-of-a-kind pieces were still being made. We built scenes using the jewelry sketches, then photographed and composited the finished pieces later.

Our CEO needed a detailed view of the proposed scenes before approving production. I used AI to develop those early references for Part 2.

Scene developed before the finished jewelry was photographed

Part 1, 2021

Frames and shadows

In Part 1, creatures emerge from museum frames as jewelry. Their shadows extend beyond the frames to show the transition from painting to physical object.

This was before we used generative AI, so the idea rested on the frames and shadows.

Marvelous Creations Part 1 catalog · 2021

Part 2, 2025

Developing the scenes with Firefly

For Part 2, the frames sit within natural landscapes, with the creatures shown as jewelry.

I used Adobe Firefly to develop compositions for review with our CEO before the pieces were finished. The references established the proposed setting and appearance of each scene.

Paper-cut settings for a flower garden, forest, pond and underwater scene

Early Firefly concepts and the final CGI scene

Firefly concept Final CGI

From concept to CGI

Using approved concepts as production references

Approved concepts became references for Leon Studio’s CGI work, covering composition, lighting and color. Once the jewelry was ready, we photographed it and composited it into the finished scenes.

AI was used for the early concepts. CGI, photography and retouching were produced with the team; I led creative direction throughout.

Firefly concept, CGI setting, product photograph and final composite

Rollout

Campaign applications

The scenes were adapted for the catalog, event invitations, press kit, event film, social media, website and newsletters.

What changed

Earlier review of the proposed scenes

High-fidelity mockups gave the internal team and local markets a clear view of the campaign before the collection was ready. This helped them plan marketing earlier and made internal approvals smoother.
